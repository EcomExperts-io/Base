What happens behind the scenes

Three situations a developer lands in, drawn as what actually runs: the person's four or five decisions in the top lane, the skills the session invokes below them, the gates that fire whether anyone remembers them in the orange lane, and the systems outside the repo at the bottom. The orange lane is the point of v3. Everything in it fired on its own during the Pique rebuild's successor, or will.

Read each figure left to right. A solid arrow is the next step; a label on it is what crosses. A dashed arc is the loop that runs until a gate or GitBot is clean.

A. A fresh rebuild

From a new client repo to a merged page. The two things that changed since v2 sit in columns three and seven: the reference is named per band before anything is fetched, and the purchase path is built and checked second, not last.

B. Joining a rebuild midway

Someone else started the store. The first act is measurement, and the tooling arrives by a three-way overlay that keeps the previous team's edits and retires what Base has renamed.

C. The rebuild is done; QA found things

Quality inspection logs issues on the Notion card. A correction is a smaller build with the same proof: the fix is verified against the band's reference, the purchase path is re-walked when it moved, the other pages are shown unchanged, and GitBot's follow-up review says whether the finding is closed.
